# Art direction

## Current decisions

The user liked the initial zombie lineup and requested more distinct faces,
body types and costumes, including one female zombie type. **Runner uses an
adult female design** in the revised proposal. The initial industrial map
proposal was rejected.

For environments, **one map** should take inspiration from the supplied image:
a snowy stone-fortress courtyard at night, towers, exterior stairs and cold
moonlight. The other **nine maps** should pursue realistic, frightening
survival horror with grounded architecture, worn materials and oppressive
lighting. The user approved [the generated fortress appearance](../assets/concepts/environments/01-fortress-courtyard.png)
on 2026-11-16. Its assignment to Quarantine Yard below is a proposed implementation choice.

Concept images are visual proposals for review. They are not finished 3D models,
rigs, animations, or screenshots of the current game. Runtime levels remain
playable blockouts while production art is developed.

Use differences in age, anatomy, posture, clothing and facial structure to make
types recognizable before relying on color. The reference sheet proposes the
first five designs; the remaining descriptions are future development briefs.

## Environment plan

| Map | Proposed environment | Horror and visual landmarks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Quarantine Yard | A quarantined snowy fortress courtyard inspired by the supplied reference. | Stone towers, exterior stairs, a sealed archway, windblown snow, cold moonlight and isolated warm windows. |
| 2. Service Tunnels | Realistic abandoned underground utility tunnels. | Wet concrete, old pipes, unreliable work lights, side passages and distant silhouettes. |
| 3. Freight Depot | Derelict rail/freight depot at night. | Rusted carriages, cargo cover, hanging lamps and heavy ground fog. |
| 4. Drainage Works | Decaying drainage and pumping complex. | Damp brick, dark water channels, corroded grilles and shadowed maintenance platforms. |
| 5. Rooftop Crossing | Abandoned urban rooftops in bad weather. | Narrow exposed connections, ventilation units, rain and distant emergency lights. |
| 6. Security Checkpoint | Deserted fortified military checkpoint. | Sandbags, abandoned vehicles, damaged searchlights and sealed quarantine gates. |
| 7. Broadcast Station | Remote disused broadcasting facility. | Broken monitors, cable-filled rooms, antenna silhouettes and weak warning lamps. |
| 8. Isolation Ward | Abandoned hospital isolation wing. | Peeling paint, stained tiles, empty beds, broken glazing and long uncertain sight lines. |
| 9. Bio-Recovery Wing | Failed biological research wing. | Observation glass, neglected equipment, condensation and cold clinical lighting. |
| 10. Breach Core | Industrial containment/reactor facility corrupted by the Breach. | Heavy machinery, containment doors, localized violet fractures and the Warden arena. |

These themes do not change level order, bounds, enemy introduction or existing
objectives. Future environment models must be adapted to the playable layouts.

## Lighting and material rules

- Aim for realistic proportions, roughness, surface wear and physically plausible light sources.
- Create fear with obscured distant spaces, restrained fog, abandonment and contrast; keep nearby traversal and threats readable.
- Reserve stronger violet effects for Breach clues, alternate barriers and objectives.
- Keep key landmarks and shared geometry recognizable when reality changes.
- Preserve escape lanes, combat cover and both navigable Shift routes.
- Avoid uniformly purple rooms, cartoon proportions and oversized decorative effects.
