# Breach Shift 2D sprite credits

The PNG atlases are local orthographic renders of the project's existing art.
No new AI generation, asset purchase or third-party download was used for this
conversion. Runtime art is 2D; the optional baker reads its immutable source
revision from the local Git repository.

- **Player and eight authored infected designs:** Breach Shift costumes and
  local materials on MakeHuman CC0 anatomy. The operator reuses the armored
  character with a distinct holding pose and colour treatment.
- **Walker and rifle:** previously generated Hyper3D Rodin assets, locally
  optimized and posed. No CC0 claim is made for these generated sources.
- **Warden:** the user's supplied sculpt, locally optimized and rigged. Its
  original provider/license was not independently verified. No CC0 claim is
  made for the supplied model or its rendered derivatives.
- **Other weapons:** original Breach Shift tactical weapon designs.

The six props are by **Quaternius**, distributed through Poly Pizza under CC0:
[barricade](https://poly.pizza/m/gLbBiYwt7l),
[broken car](https://poly.pizza/m/Y67erogmR9),
[crate](https://poly.pizza/m/3VGWnZPXmG),
[barrel](https://poly.pizza/m/1orHe0kCc1),
[street light](https://poly.pizza/m/nFwrlcLvM5) and
[debris](https://poly.pizza/m/WrIiMMxyEP).

Four 256px base-colour tiles were extracted from the existing embedded
Poly Haven PBR textures: [Asphalt 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_02)
by Rob Tuytel; [Concrete Wall 008](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_wall_008)
by Charlotte Baglioni and Dario Barresi; [Brick Wall 07](https://polyhaven.com/a/brick_wall_07)
by Dimitrios Savva; and [Rusty Metal 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/rusty_metal_03)
by Amal Kumar. These sources were acquired under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license).

The side-view maps, parallax scenery, signs, interface and effects are original
Breach Shift work. CoD maps, third-party mod installers, remote services and
credentials are not part of the game.
